## 🔥 CRITICAL FIXES IMPLEMENTED

### **1. AIRDROP RESTRUCTURE ⚠️**

**Original Problem:**
- 15% (150M NTIQ) immediate release = massive selling pressure

**New Solution:**
```
Total Airdrop: 15% (150M NTIQ)

Phase 1 - Immediate Release: 5% (50M NTIQ)
├─ Early adopters (Wave 1-2): 2% (20M)
├─ Beta testers: 1.5% (15M)
├─ Community builders: 1% (10M)
└─ Buildathons participants: 0.5% (5M)

Phase 2 - Vested Release: 10% (100M NTIQ)
├─ Linear unlock over 12 months
├─ 8.33M NTIQ per month
├─ No cliff (starts immediately)
└─ Can be claimed monthly
```

**Impact:**
- ✅ 67% reduction in immediate circulation (150M → 50M)
- ✅ Reduced selling pressure by spreading over 12 months
- ✅ Encourages long-term holding

```

Max Wallet: 10,000,000 NTIQ (1% of supply)
├─ Prevents single-entity control
├─ Encourages distribution
└─ Exempt for DAO treasury, staking contracts

Early Selling Penalty (Optional - Wave 6):
├─ Sell within 30 days: 10% penalty
├─ Sell within 60 days: 5% penalty
├─ After 60 days: No penalty
└─ Penalties → additional burn
```

**Impact:**
- ✅ Prevents whale manipulation
- ✅ Encourages long-term holding
- ✅ More stable price action

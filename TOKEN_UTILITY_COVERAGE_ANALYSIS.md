## 🚫 **CRITICAL FEATURE GAPS** (Need New Utilities)

### **GAP #1: Prediction Insurance** 🆕

**Problem:** Users have no way to protect their stakes from losses

**Current State:**
- User stakes 1,000 NTIQ
- Prediction is wrong
- User loses 1,000 NTIQ
- No protection mechanism

**Proposed Solution:**
```
NEW UTILITY #11: Prediction Insurance

HOW IT WORKS:
1. User makes prediction (stake: 1,000 NTIQ)
2. User purchases insurance (cost: 50-100 NTIQ)
3. If prediction loses, user gets 50% stake back (500 NTIQ)
4. If prediction wins, insurance fee is lost (but user won anyway)

BENEFITS:
• Risk mitigation for users
• Additional NTIQ utility
• Platform revenue from insurance fees
• Encourages larger stakes (users feel safer)

ECONOMICS:
• Insurance Cost: 5-10% of stake amount
• Payout: 50% of stake if loss
• Platform keeps: 50% of insurance fees
• DAO Treasury: 50% of insurance fees

EXAMPLE:
Stake 1,000 NTIQ on BTC UP
Buy insurance: 100 NTIQ
Total investment: 1,100 NTIQ

Scenario A (WIN):
- Get back: 1,800 NTIQ (1.8x multiplier)
- Insurance lost: 100 NTIQ
- Net profit: +700 NTIQ

Scenario B (LOSS):
- Get back: 500 NTIQ (insurance payout)
- Total loss: -600 NTIQ (instead of -1,000)
- Risk reduced by 40%
```

**Implementation Priority:** 🔥 **HIGH**

### **GAP #3: Multiplier Boost Purchase** 🆕

**Problem:** Users cannot enhance their potential winnings beyond base multipliers

**Current State:**
- Fixed multipliers (1.8x for single, 2-50x for TrendRide)
- No way to increase potential rewards
- Users want more control

**Proposed Solution:**
```
NEW UTILITY #13: Multiplier Boost

HOW IT WORKS:
1. User makes prediction with base multiplier
2. User can purchase boost (costs 10-20% of stake)
3. Multiplier increases by 0.1x - 0.5x
4. If win, user gets boosted reward
5. If lose, boost fee is also lost

BOOST TIERS:
• Bronze Boost (+0.1x): 10% of stake
• Silver Boost (+0.2x): 15% of stake  
• Gold Boost (+0.3x): 20% of stake
• Platinum Boost (+0.5x): 30% of stake

BENEFITS:
• More control over risk/reward
• Differentiated gameplay
• Advanced strategy options
• Additional NTIQ utility

ECONOMICS:
• Boost cost is NON-REFUNDABLE
• 40% goes to Prize Pool
• 30% BURNED
• 30% to DAO Treasury

EXAMPLE:
Base prediction: 1,000 NTIQ @ 1.8x
Purchase Gold Boost: 200 NTIQ (+0.3x)
Total invested: 1,200 NTIQ
New multiplier: 2.1x

Win Scenario:
- Payout: 1,000 × 2.1 = 2,100 NTIQ
- Net profit: +900 NTIQ (vs +800 without boost)

Loss Scenario:
- Lost: 1,200 NTIQ (vs 1,000 without boost)
- Extra risk: -200 NTIQ
```
